## Demonstration evidence to collect

| Requirement | Evidence | Limits to disclose |
|---|---|---|
| Profile intake | Upload a selected synthetic CV, review skills and confirm intake | Human confirmation required; do not promise perfect parsing. |
| Updatable signals | Snapshot, sources, observation period and publication record | Worker inference does not prove publication; replay is historical. |
| Matching | Ranked roles, score components and skills gap | Record actual values; scenario/ranking work is owned separately. |
| Pathway | Saved ordered courses, sources, duration, cost and uncovered gaps | Short modules do not establish job readiness. |
| SDG mapping | Cited targets and reasons for role/skill alignment | Curated alignment is not measured UN indicator achievement. |
| Uncertainty | Moderate/fast assumptions and estimate labels | Heuristic ranges are not calibrated confidence intervals. |
| Organisation | Synthetic membership, sharing choice and programme acceptance | People retain personnel and learning decisions. |
